All Status Types

Developer reference

An exhaustive map of every state each field in the product table can take, for hand-off to development. The status library below catalogues each field’s possible states using the Merchsmith icon system. Labels can be shown under each icon, hidden, or surfaced on hover.

Notes for development

  • When a Product is added to a Project the default status is Idea. At this stage, samples can be ordered. Once work begins the status moves to In Progress and the other fields advance through their statuses.
    • ve to the next statuses shown.
  • When the Design or Price move to Awaiting Approval, the main product status moves to In Progress. It stays there until all statuses are Approved and the Delivery Date is confirmed by the ACM.
  • The red action icon swaps depending on whether the user is the Buyer, Distributor or Supplier.
  • Delivery Date is chosen by the customer and the deadline is generated automatically. Customers cannot choose a date sooner than the lead time, or while stock is unconfirmed. ACMs can override the lead-time and deadlines that are usually automated. There is also a no delivery date option.
  • Field labels can be shown beneath the icons, hidden, or revealed on hover — toggle above.
  • Re-order option is available once a product has been Ordered.
  • The customer assigns a delivery date; Design and Sample will always show as Approved at that point.
  • If a product Price has expired, the Price status reverts to Being Checked.
  • If a product is not in stock, the Stock status reverts to Being Checked.
  • If the Price is valid and the product is in stock, Price and Stock show as Approved and In Stock. In all such cases the Delivery Date shows as Being Checked.
  • Unbranded lead-time differs from the branded lead-time.
