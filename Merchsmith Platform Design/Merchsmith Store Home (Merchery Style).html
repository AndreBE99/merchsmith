Frequently asked questions

Everything on pricing, lead times, sampling and delivery — the things that matter before you order.

View all FAQs
Why do prices vary within a range?
Unit price depends on quantity and branding — more units and simpler decoration lower the per-unit cost. Every product shows a live price matrix so you see exactly where you land.
What's the minimum order quantity?
MOQ varies by product and supplier — typically from 25–250 units. It's shown on every product and in the database.
Can you shorten the production lead time?
Often yes. Standard lead times are listed per product; ask your account team about our no-fail express option for tight deadlines.
Can you ship to multiple addresses?
Yes — split delivery across sites or individuals, with per-address tracking.
How sustainable are the products?
Every product carries a 0–10 sustainability score based on materials and origin, so you can choose with confidence — no greenwashing.
