Card payments, fees & invoice documents

How customers and distributors pay pro formas and VAT invoices by card or bank transfer, how the card transaction fee is charged without changing an issued VAT invoice, and how the pro forma, VAT invoice, card fee invoice and credit note documents are built.

1. The rules

A customer may choose bank transfer or be on account when they order, so no card fee is added. If they later pay by card, the fee has to be charged. Issued VAT invoices can’t be changed, so the fee is never added to one. These rules were agreed with Merchsmith:

  1. Commercial cards only. Distributors can’t accept personal cards, so every card payment carries the fee. Personal cards are declined.
  2. The fee has its own VAT invoice, raised at the moment a card payment succeeds (tax point = payment date). Failed or abandoned payments raise nothing.
  3. Pro forma switched to card → a revision. The same number with a suffix (PF-53003-R2); the previous one is marked superseded. Switching back to bank transfer issues another revision.
  4. Part payments are allowed. An invoice can be part paid; the balance carries on and the fee applies only to the amount paid by card.
  5. Refunds return the fee in proportion. A credit note against a card-paid invoice refunds that share of the fee.
  6. Pay links work without logging in: secure, expiring, view and pay only.
  7. Checkout stays as it is: a card payment at checkout still adds the fee to the order total. It now runs the same commercial-card check.
Legal: UK rules ban surcharging consumer-type cards. Charging only commercial cards, at no more than the processor’s cost, is the basis of these rules. Merchsmith is confirming this with its advisers. The fee also carries 20% VAT, as it’s part of the price of the supply.

3. Payment flows

Every flow below lands on the same pay page and records one payment in the ledger.

A · Upfront order, switch to card
  1. Order placed with bank transfer; pro forma PF-53003 awaiting payment.
  2. Order page → Pay by card instead → confirm.
  3. MSPAY.revise(o,'card') issues PF-53003-R2 (fee shown), logs “superseded” in the order activity, opens the pay page with R2 ticked.
  4. Card payment succeeds → order marked paid, fee invoice INV-1044-F1 raised.
B · On account, pay invoices by card
  1. Each product’s VAT invoice is raised at dispatch (unchanged).
  2. Customer opens the pay link, Finance → Invoices, or the order’s “Invoices to pay”.
  3. Ticks one or more invoices, optionally edits the amount (part payment), pays by commercial card.
  4. One fee invoice for the whole payment; each invoice is paid or part paid.
C · Bank transfer from the pay page
  1. Choose Bank transfer: bank details and one combined reference (PAY-xxxxxx).
  2. “I’ve sent the transfer” records a pending payment. No fee.
  3. Documents are marked paid when the distributor records the money in Finance (unchanged flow).
D · Checkout (unchanged)
  1. Card at checkout adds the fee to the order total, as before.
  2. New: “Business (commercial) cards only” and a card-type check on Place order. A personal card blocks with a message.

4. The card fee

  • Rate: from Finance → Integrations → Stripe (MSINT.stripe().fee.uk / .intl), default 1.5% UK/EU and 3% outside Europe. The payer picks the card region on the pay page; the live build should read it from the card.
  • Base: the amount being paid by card in this payment (the sum of the ticked amounts), not the documents’ full totals.
  • Gross to net: the fee is treated as VAT-inclusive: gross = amount × rate, net = gross ÷ 1.2, vat = gross − net.
  • Numbering: INV-<order no>-F<n>, using the first paid document’s order; n counts fee invoices already raised for that order.
  • Shown before paying: selected total, “Card payment fee (1.5%, inc. VAT)”, total to pay. The Pay button shows the total.
Example
£1,000 part of INV-1042-01 + £2,628 for PF-53003-R2 = £3,628 by card → fee £54.42 (net £45.35 + VAT £9.07) on INV-1042-F1.
Distributor absorbs the fee
If Stripe is set to absorb, checkout charges no fee. The pay page still uses the configured % (prototype); the live build should respect the setting (see Going live).

5. Pro forma revisions

  • Numbers: pro formas are numbered in sequence from PF-53001 (MSPRO.number('order:'+id), register ms-pf-register). The checkout pro forma number is adopted by the order when it’s placed, so it never changes.
  • Revisions: ms-pf-rev[orderId] holds [{rev, at, method, by, supersedes}]. MSPAY.pfNo(o) returns PF-53003 or PF-53003-R2.
  • R2 document: shows the revision note (“R2 issued …, replaces PF-53003 (pay by card)”), the card fee, “Total to pay by card”, and a “Pay by business card” box. Bank details stay, as bank transfer is still possible without the fee.
  • Paying a superseded number: money received against an earlier revision still counts towards the order (outstanding() sums all revisions).

6. Part payments

  • Balance: MSPAY.paidOn(doc) sums every paid ledger item for that document. Balance = document total − paid.
  • Status: “Part paid” on the pay page, the VAT invoice (pill, Payment status box, “Invoice total / Paid to date / Balance due”), Finance → Customer invoices (chip and “£x paid · £y left”) and Customer Finance.
  • Fully paid: when the paid amount reaches the total, ms-order-ops[id].lines[i].invPaid (VAT invoice) or x.paid (pro forma) is set.
  • Due dates and reminders carry on against the balance. Finance’s Outstanding figure counts the balance, not the total.

7. Refunds & credit notes

  • Credit notes are their own document (D10). VAT invoices no longer list credit notes.
  • Fee refund: if the invoice being credited was paid by card, the credit note adds a line “Card payment fee refund” = MSPAY.feeShare(invoice) × (credit gross ÷ invoice gross), capped at 100%. It’s added to the credit’s net, VAT and total.
  • Fee share: each card payment’s fee is shared across the documents it paid in proportion to the amount paid on each.
  • How it’s settled (shown on the credit note): on account → taken off what they owe or held as credit; card → refunded to the card; bank transfer → refunded within 5 working days.

9. Documents

All four are produced by one renderer, MSPRO.render(data) in proforma-doc.js, which returns a full A4 HTML document. kind picks the document.

Pro forma (D05 checkout, D07 order) · default kind
Awaiting payment / Paid, revision note, card fee block. Preview
VAT invoice (D08 / D08P) · kind:'vat'
One per product at dispatch. Unpaid / Part paid / Paid / Overdue; variant and SKU rows; its own charges and delivery. Preview
Card fee invoice · kind:'vat'
Built by MSPAY.feeDoc(payment): one line “Card payment fee”, notes listing what the payment covered, Paid.
Credit note (D10) · kind:'credit'
Against invoice, reason, negative lines, fee refund line, how it’s settled. Preview

Layout

  • Real A4 pages (@page{size:A4;margin:0}, section.page 210 × 297mm). An inline paginator moves blocks into pages; a product never splits across pages.
  • Repeated on every page: header (logo, document title, number, VAT reg / “not a VAT invoice”, status pill, Page n / N), reference strip, Invoice to / Customer contact / Payment status row, item column headings, company footer.
  • Per product: number, name, SKU, qty, unit, amount; Project, Branding, Colours, Deliver by; variant rows (VAT invoice); Deliver to cards (split deliveries numbered with colour × qty, date and delivery notes); additional charges and delivery as their own rows.

Where the data comes from

  • Distributor: Organisation profile (legal name, company no., VAT no., address, contact), PDF or header logo (Branding), colourway (--periwinkle-deep), bank (Finance → Bank details).
  • Customer: billing address, invoice contact, VAT number (new field in Organisations → Edit terms, ms-cust-account[id].vatNo), contact phone from their team record.
  • Lines: new order lines carry the store SKU, delivery notes from checkout (and new-address instructions), charges with qty × unit, and the project.

10. Finance tables

  • VAT invoice totals come from MSPAY.orderMoney(o), so they include each product’s own charges and delivery; order-level leftovers (older orders’ single delivery figure, rounding) stay on the last item dispatched.
  • New: Part paid status and chip, “Card fee invoices” type and chip, card-paid invoices shown as Paid, Outstanding counts balances.
  • Distributor actions: card icon per unpaid row and bulk “Take card payment” (one customer at a time) open the pay page with those documents ticked.
  • Accounting sync: on a successful card payment, MSINT.sync('inv', fee.no) and MSINT.sync('pay', payment.id) register the fee invoice and the payment; fee rows show the sync icon.

14. Going live

Stripe
  • Use Stripe Connect on the distributor’s account; collect cards with Payment Element.
  • Commercial check from the card’s funding / product data before confirming; decline personal cards server-side.
  • Card region from the card country, not the payer’s choice.
  • Raise the fee invoice only from the payment_intent.succeeded webhook.
  • Respect “absorb fee” in Integrations.
Backend
  • Payments, allocations (payment ↔ document, amount) and fee invoices as tables; balances computed server-side.
  • Invoice and pro forma numbering in one transaction-safe sequence.
  • Pay links: signed token (customer, documents, expiry 30 days), view and pay only, rate-limited.
  • Receipt emails to the payer (guest email or customer contact).
Accounting (Xero)
  • Post the fee invoice as its own sales invoice.
  • Post one payment allocated across every document it paid, including part payments.
  • Credit notes with the fee refund line allocated against the fee invoice.
Still to decide
  • Legal confirmation of commercial-card surcharging and the fee cap.
  • Whether bank transfers from the pay page auto-match via the bank feed.
  • Fee handling when a card payment is refunded by Stripe (not via a credit note).

15. Try it

  1. Open MS-1044 (Citrix, bank transfer, unpaid). In the Summary, click Pay by card instead and confirm. The pro forma becomes PF-…-R2.
  2. Download the pro forma to see the revision note and the card fee.
  3. On the pay page, also tick INV-1042-01, change its amount to 1000, enter a card number such as 4242 4242 4242 4242 and pay. Try 4000… to see the personal-card decline.
  4. Download the fee invoice from the receipt. Back on the order, see the paid status and the fee invoice row.
  5. Open Finance → Customer invoices: INV-1042-01 is Part paid; filter Card fee invoices.
  6. As a distributor, use the card icon on any unpaid row to open “Take a card payment”. Add ?guest=1 to the pay page to see the emailed-link view.

Data is stored in this browser. Download a backup first (Admin → Settings → Data backup) if you want to keep the current state.
