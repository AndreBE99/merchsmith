Full price matrix & how the unit price is built

Opened from a “Show price table” button under the Price slider. The matrix is the supplier's cost price by quantity break and method; the slider's customer unit price is derived from it via markup, with delivery handled separately.

Mock-up only — illustrative figures. Nothing live has changed.

Top row is the unbranded item. Each method below shows the per-unit decoration cost by quantity break, with rows per number of print colours where relevant, and the supplier's setup charge. These are cost figures from the supplier — the customer never sees this table.

Locked prices — distributor override

The distributor can lock specific prices against a product's costs for a chosen branding across a selected quantity window. Within that window the locked prices override the matrix; quantities above or below still use the matrix as normal.

These locked prices apply only to this project, product & branding — they are not site-wide and do not carry to other projects, products or branding setups.
The customer only sees the locked quantities chosen — any breaks still using the standard matrix price are hidden, so the customer's quantity range is restricted to the locked window.

2From cost to the customer's unit price

The unit price in the slider is the sell price. It's built from the supplier total cost (item + decoration + setup), a banded markup on that total, then divided back to a per-unit figure. Setup is absorbed into the unit sell price, not shown as a line.

⚑ Once a price is approved it is locked and cannot be changed — to amend it, the customer must reopen it (Request changes) and approve the new price again. Prices expire after 30 days by default (distributor-configurable); on expiry the line returns to Being checked for re-quoting.

3Delivery — separate, scales with quantity

Delivery is not in the unit price — it's a separate line that rises with quantity (more boxes / pallets) as the slider moves. The supplier delivery cost is marked up 25% and rounded to the nearest £.

Setup charge: marked up ×1.35 and rounded up to the nearest £5 (e.g. £25.00 cost → £33.75 → £35.00 sell). Like delivery, it's a one-off and not part of the per-unit price, but it is spread into the unit sell price shown in the locked matrix.

setup sell = roundUpTo5( setupCost × 1.35 )

5Distributor discounts

The distributor can apply discounts that reduce the customer sell price (and their profit). They stack/resolve by specificity, most-specific winning. Some are defined in Distributor settings, others set on the project/product.

8How it drives the slider

  1. The chosen method + colour count (from the branding builder) selects the matrix row; the slider quantity selects the break column.
  2. Item + decoration + setup at that break → total supplier cost.
  3. Apply the markup band for that cost (respecting the min-profit floor) → total sell, ÷ qty → unit sell price shown on the slider and in the customer's table.
  4. Delivery is calculated separately (×1.25, rounded) and shown as its own line — it doesn't affect the unit price.
  5. Any quantity that falls between breaks uses the closest break below the chosen quantity — for both the unit price and the delivery cost.

9Sample pricing

Samples are priced per type (unbranded and branded). Each sample's cost can be a fixed price, a quote requested from the supplier, or free. The sell price applies a markup rule — the same shape as setup costs: ×1.35, minimum +£5 uplift.

In the modal — Sample tab

  • A branded sample still requires the design to be approved before it can be ordered.
