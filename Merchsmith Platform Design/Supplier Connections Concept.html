Supplier connections & product feeds

How a distributor connects to the suppliers whose products stock their store. Distributors invite the suppliers they already work with; the supplier confirms; their products then become available to add to that distributor's store. A name-match guard stops distributors discovering and connecting to other distributors' suppliers they don't already know.

Concept only — nothing here is wired into the live platform yet. For you to react to before we build.

1The connection flow

A supplier's products never appear in a distributor's store until an explicit, confirmed connection exists between them.

1

Distributor searches

Finds the supplier they already work with on Merchsmith — or uploads a CSV of their supplier list.

2

Name-match guard

The supplier only shows if the search matches ≥80% of their registered name (see §2).

3

Request to connect

Distributor sends a partner request. Supplier is notified by email + on their Merchsmith enquiries dashboard.

4

Supplier confirms

Supplier accepts the partnership — they choose to make their catalogue available to that distributor.

5

Products available

The supplier's products become addable to that distributor's store (API feed or manual).

2The 80% name-match guard

To stop a distributor browsing and poaching another distributor's suppliers, search only surfaces a supplier on a two-signal guard — the query must match at least 80% of the supplier's registered company name or the distributor supplies the supplier's email domain. The distributor effectively has to already know who they're looking for, while accidental near-name collisions don't leak a supplier.

Bag… (partial query)Hidden — 34% name, no domain match
Below 80%

Recommended algorithm: token-set similarity (compare on words, ignoring order and corporate suffixes like "Ltd / Limited / UK") scored 0–100, threshold ≥80 — this tolerates dropped suffixes, trading names and word reordering far better than raw character distance. Combine with an exact email-domain match as an alternative pass: if the distributor knows the supplier's domain they're clearly a genuine contact, so surface it even if the typed name is loose. A vague single word matches neither and returns nothing.

4When the supplier isn't on Merchsmith

From a "not found" result, the distributor can still bring the supplier on board — two ways, with different capabilities.

Path A

Add it yourself

The distributor creates the supplier record themselves and manages the relationship privately.

Manual product uploads (CSV / one-by-one)
No live API feed
No automated stock / price sync

Best for: small or occasional suppliers, or getting started fast.

Path B

Request Merchsmith onboards them

Recommended

The distributor asks Merchsmith to formally add the supplier to the platform. Merchsmith invites & verifies them and sets up the integration.

API connection / live product feed
Automated stock & price sync
Supplier gets their own Merchsmith account & enquiries dashboard

Best for: core suppliers a distributor orders from regularly.

5How products reach the store · access & revocation

  • Connected (API): the supplier's normalised feed flows in; the distributor chooses which products to publish to their store (and sets their own pricing/markup on top).
  • Connected (manual): products the distributor uploaded for that supplier; no automatic updates — the distributor maintains them.
  • Per-distributor isolation: a connection is between one distributor and one supplier. Another distributor seeing the same supplier in search still has to request & be confirmed independently — connections and catalogues are never shared between distributors.
  • Decline & revoke: a supplier can decline a request, or later revoke a distributor's access. If products are already published, the distributor is warned of the issue, and those products are marked Discontinued — they stay visible for reference but cannot be added to a project.
  • Admin only: only a distributor admin can search, request, add or onboard suppliers (for now).

6Resolved decisions

  • Match guard: token-set name similarity ≥80% (suffix-insensitive) OR exact email-domain match — prevents accidental collisions without blocking someone who genuinely knows the supplier.
  • CSV upload: resolves to a found / not-found results page; bulk "request all" for matches, Path A/B per unmatched row.
  • Decline / revoke: allowed; published products → warned + marked discontinued + unusable in projects.
  • Manual → API upgrade: when a Path-A supplier later joins formally, the live feed reconciles with the manual catalogue (manual entries map to feed items where possible, the rest retained).
  • Permissions: admin only, for now.
