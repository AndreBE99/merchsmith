Organisations & users

How organisations, teams, people and roles fit together, who can do what, and how a person moves from invitation to deactivation. The permissions tables below are rendered live from the same matrix the app enforces, so this page cannot drift from the code.

1Organisation types

Every person belongs to exactly one organisation. Merchsmith operates the platform; each distributor runs an install for its customers; suppliers feed products. Customers and suppliers never see each other.

2Entities

The shape the backend needs. In the prototype these live in org-data.js (seeds) plus localStorage('ms-people') (every edit), read through window.MSPEOPLE.

3A person's lifecycle

Invited→ accepts link →Active→ admin deactivates →Deactivated→ admin reactivates →Active
  • No deletion. People are deactivated, never removed. Their history, comments and project work stay attributed to them; the seat is freed and they cannot sign in.
  • Revoking an invitation moves the pending user to deactivated, so the link stops working and the attempt stays on record.
  • Every organisation keeps at least one active Admin (Owner on the Merchsmith team). Deactivating or demoting the last one is blocked.
  • Email is unique within an organisation. The same person may exist in two organisations with separate records.

4Who can do what

Rendered from MSPEOPLE.PERMS. Everyone can always edit their own profile (name, title, contact, photo, preferences) but not their own role. Merchsmith operators act across every organisation.
