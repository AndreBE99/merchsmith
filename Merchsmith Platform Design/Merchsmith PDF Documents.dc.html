How they work today. Most order documents use one shared A4 template (printDoc in order-detail.js): a header, a simple table and the seller box, opened in a viewer with Download PDF (the browser’s print → Save as PDF). That’s why so many sit at “Basic layout”: they share one plain design. Improving that template lifts D06–D16 together; each then needs its own details checked.

Suggested order of work

  1. One document design system for D05–D16: shared header (distributor logo, document type, number, dates), address blocks, line table, totals and footer (company, VAT and bank details). Brings 12 documents up together.
  2. D08 / D08P VAT invoice, unpaid and paid first, as they have legal requirements, then D07 / D05 pro forma, D10 credit note, D09 additional invoice.
  3. D12 Purchase order (the most detailed), then D13–D15.
  4. D06 Order confirmation and D16 Subscription invoice.
  5. D17 / D18 reports: proper print layouts for the dashboards.
  6. D01 as the quote: add quote number, wording and terms.
  7. D21 Statement of account and D23 Supplier remittance advice.
