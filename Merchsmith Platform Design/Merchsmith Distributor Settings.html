Lead times

Days in lieu

Every product has a supplier lead time from the supplier's data. The customer lead time you quote adds a buffer of working days — "days in lieu" — to leave room for issues. This is the account-wide default; individual suppliers can override it from their profile.

Logos

Square, header & PDF

Every logo for your organisation lives here. The square logo is used in lists, chat and avatars; the header logo appears top-left in the app; the PDF logo is used on proofs, quotes and order documents.

Square logo
Used in lists, chat and avatars. Square, at least 256 × 256 px.
Favicon
Browser tab, bookmarks and home-screen shortcuts for you and your customers. Square PNG or SVG, at least 64 × 64 px.
Platform header logo
Tone
Shown top-left. Transparent PNG or SVG recommended. Recommended: 320 × 80 px (4:1), max 512 px wide. Double-click to crop & reposition.
PDF / document logo
Tone
Used on proofs, quotes & order docs. Recommended: 600 × 200 px (3:1) or vector SVG, transparent background. Double-click to crop & reposition.

Your subscription

—

Your plan includes the whole platform — nothing is held back on a lower tier. The only thing that changes between plans is the monthly base, how many distributor seats are included, and the rate for each seat beyond that.

What counts as a seat

A seat is one billable user in your own team. Your customers and your suppliers log in free — they never count towards your allowance and are never billed, on any plan.

If you add more people

Compare the tiers against your current team size. Seats are billed monthly at the rate for your plan.
