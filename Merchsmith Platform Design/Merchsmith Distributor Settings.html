Distributor settings

Account-wide settings for Mojo Promotions. These apply across every customer and project unless overridden at a more specific level.

Lead times

Days in lieu

Every product has a supplier lead time from the supplier's data. The customer lead time you quote adds a buffer of working days — "days in lieu" — to leave room for issues. This is the account-wide default; individual suppliers can override it from their profile.

Default days in lieu
3
working days
Customer lead time = supplier lead time + 3 working days in lieu
Per-supplier overrides now live on each supplier's profile in Organisations → Suppliers, not here.
