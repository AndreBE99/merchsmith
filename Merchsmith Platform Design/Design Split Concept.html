Splitting Design into Visual + Proof

Today "Design" is a single approval. In reality there are two distinct artefacts the customer signs off — the Visual (an indicative render of how it will look, producible early without full branding detail) and the Proof (the exact, to-scale technical drawing that is the binding artefact). This concept proposes splitting them into two approval stages that can run in sequence or in parallel, with a reworked status set.

This is a mock-up only — nothing in the live Dashboard, Gallery or modal has been changed. It's here for you to react to before we build.

1The two stages

Each stage is its own short approval cycle. The Visual usually leads (stage 1) and gives the customer early confidence on look & feel; the Proof follows (stage 2) for technical sign-off — but the proof can start before the visual is approved when timelines are tight, so they may overlap.

4How the modal's Design area could present it

Two directions for the modal. Both make the Visual → Proof relationship explicit; they differ in how much space they give each stage.

Option A — Two stacked rowsRecommended

Visual and Proof each get their own row with status + inline action. Clearest for the parallel/overlap case.

Each row carries its own visual/proof preview + the approve action. The customer can approve the visual early, then the proof later.

Option B — One extended track

A single 4-step progress: Visual draft → Visual approved → Proof → Proof approved. Simpler, but assumes visual always precedes proof.

5Decisions I'd need from you

  • Is the Visual always required, or optional / skippable for repeat or simple lines (jump straight to Proof)?
  • Can Proof begin before Visual approval? (Determines Option A vs B — A supports it, B doesn't.)
  • Does Visual approval gate anything, or is it advisory? (e.g. must the visual be approved before a branded sample can be ordered?)
  • Roll-up label: in the table, show the active stage ("Approve visual" / "Approve proof") as drawn here — or always say "Design" with a sub-line?
  • Versioning: independent Visual vN and Proof vN counters (as shown), or one shared Design version?
